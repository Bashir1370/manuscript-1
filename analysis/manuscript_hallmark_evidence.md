# Current manuscript Hallmark evidence

This branch uses the three-study workflow documented in [three_dataset_protocol.md](three_dataset_protocol.md), with original source blobs in [three_dataset_input_provenance.csv](three_dataset_input_provenance.csv).

The shared-significant selection is recalculated over all 50 Hallmarks: 10 positive and two negative sets satisfy NES concordance and FDR <0.05 in all three studies. Opposite directions are selected independently of FDR. Leading-edge analysis follows the new 10-pathway positive selection. Use only the canonical three-study output directories listed in the protocol for manuscript summaries.

# OIPN-required gene prioritization
Decision date: 2026-09-30. Input snapshot: `3e0f3fa94a350c21b1de3495d4c879d551d723b1`.
This is an explicitly post hoc, exploratory prioritization of the existing three-study leading-edge genes, agreed after reviewing the results.

## Selection
Input: `results/shared_Hallmark_leading_edge_three_dataset/shared_all3.csv`.
A gene must:
1. Belong to the original leading edge of the **same** selected positive Hallmark in OIPN GSE160543, NC GSE246156 and CCI GSE212311 (at least one such pathway).
2. Have original log2FC >0 in all three studies.
3. Have original genome-wide DE gene FDR <0.05 in **OIPN GSE160543**.
4. Have original DE gene FDR <0.05 in **NC or CCI, or both**.

The input covers the ten GSEA-shared positive Hallmarks. Pathway selection and all-three leading-edge membership are unchanged. Original within-study gene FDR is retained: no BH correction over 78 or 20 genes, no pooled effect/FDR, and no added log2FC magnitude threshold.
Missing gene FDR is not significant. Repeated symbols are counted once, after checking identical original effect/FDR across their pathway rows.

## Results
- Existing all-three leading-edge genes: **78** unique genes, 109 pathway-gene memberships.
- Positive log2FC in all three: **78**.
- Gene FDR <0.05 in at least two studies, allowing any pair: **25**.
- New OIPN-required selection: **20** unique genes, **32** shared pathway-gene memberships.
- Strict subset significant in all three: **8**.
- Additional selected genes significant in exactly two: **12**; all are significant in OIPN and NC. Their CCI log2FC is positive but gene FDR does not meet 0.05.
- Excluded NC+CCI-only significant genes: **5** (Bcl3, Ifi30, Il2rb, Spi1, Tspo). They remain in the 78-gene evidence set; exclusion from this priority list does not mean biological irrelevance.

Strict eight: Bard1, Ccnf, Cdkn1a, Csf1, Il4r, Itgal, Kif22, Mcm5.
Additional twelve: Cenpf, Col16a1, Ezh2, Icam1, Kif11, Mybl2, Plk1, Prc1, Pttg1, Rhbdf2, Top2a, Tpx2.

## Interpretation
Report as shared leading-edge genes with positive effects in all three studies and gene-level significance in OIPN plus at least one physical-injury cohort. Keep the strict eight identifiable; do not describe all 20 as significant in CCI or all three studies.
This is a descriptive prioritization rule, not a joint FDR-controlled replication test. Selection used the same data as GSEA and is not independent validation. Pathway overlap counts are not independent replication, network centrality or mechanistic evidence. The post hoc exclusion of GSE126773 and bulk-DRG cell-origin limitations still apply.

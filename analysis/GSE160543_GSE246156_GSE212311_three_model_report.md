# Three-model rat DRG Hallmark comparison: OIPN, NC and CCI

## Inputs and scope

This compares independently analyzed bulk DRG contrasts. It does not pool count matrices or treat samples from separate studies as a single design.

| Model | GEO | Contrast | Biological samples | Tissue/time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| OIPN | GSE160543 | Oxaliplatin minus vehicle | 4 versus 4 | Rat DRG; level/time not documented to match NC or CCI |
| NC | GSE246156 | L5 compression minus sham | 3 versus 3 | Rat L5 DRG, day 7 |
| CCI | GSE212311 | CCI minus sham | 3 versus 3 | Rat ipsilateral L4–L6 DRG, day 11 |

Inputs are the three study-level `GSEA_Hallmark_results.csv` files in the repository/local results tree. The CCI input was reviewed from the author's `pathway_analysis_source_aware.zip`; it is generated by `scripts/GSE212311/06_GSE212311_source_aware_pathways.R`. OIPN and NC results are committed under their respective `results/` directories. The reproducible comparison script is `scripts/cross_model/01_three_model_Hallmark_comparison.R`.

Each input contains the same 50 rat Hallmark pathways, with signed DESeq2 Wald statistic rankings, fgsea (10,000 permutations), set-size limits 15–500, and within-study BH correction. Recorded sessions used R 4.5.2, msigdbr 26.1.1 and fgsea 1.36.2. Gene feature inventories and mapping rules differ; compare direction and within-study evidence, not the numerical NES difference as a formal interaction test.

## Main result

FDR < 0.05: **30/50 OIPN**, **33/50 NC**, **17/50 CCI**. Twelve pathways reach FDR < 0.05 in **all three**, and all twelve have concordant signed NES. The twelve are allograft rejection, apoptosis, coagulation, epithelial–mesenchymal transition, fatty-acid metabolism, G2M checkpoint, IL6/JAK/STAT3 signaling, interferon-alpha response, interferon-gamma response, oxidative phosphorylation, p53 pathway, and TNFA/NFKB signaling. Hallmark names are gene-set labels; they do not establish a literal transplant, epithelial transition, or cell cycle in a defined cell type.

An FDR pattern is not an interaction test. E2F and mitotic spindle are supported in OIPN and NC but not CCI; this does not establish absence or specificity in CCI. Likewise, the broad inflammatory-response set misses FDR 0.05 in OIPN, although its NES is positive and the more specific TNFA, IL6 and interferon programs are supported in all three.

## Leading-edge overlap and interpretation

Among pathways significant in all three, the shared leading-edge intersection contains 19 names for G2M (including Mcm5, Ccnf, Kif22, Top2a and Plk1), 34 for oxidative phosphorylation, 18 for interferon-gamma, 15 for TNFA/NFKB, and eight for IL6/JAK/STAT3. These are descriptive overlaps of related gene sets and do not identify the cell type producing the signal.

CCI source-aware gene-level data show Cdkn1a upregulated (FDR 0.00117), whereas Cdk1 is not significant (FDR 0.894). Cdc20 was excluded from the strict CCI pathway mapping because its GEO `trans_type` combines retained-intron and protein-coding labels; its tested MSTRG feature has FDR 0.720. These facts do not support a claim that Cdk1, Cdc20 and Cdkn1a jointly rise in post-mitotic neurons. The consistent immune and oxidative-phosphorylation patterns are tissue-level injury-associated signatures. The CCI GO results additionally show increased immune terms and decreased synaptic/axon terms, but GO universes and workflows are not harmonized across all three, so they are not included in the three-way Hallmark tally.

## Limits and next gate

The OIPN complete-case import excludes genes missing from any selected GEO file, including some high-count genes. NC has a broad transcriptional and count-composition shift. CCI uses stringent, source-aware RGD protein-coding MSTRG labels and excludes ambiguous names. The studies differ in injury mechanism, DRG level and sampling time; CCI and NC have only three biological libraries per group. Non-significance in one study is not evidence of model specificity, and bulk data cannot establish neuronal cell-cycle re-entry, senescence, or a causal pain mechanism.

A manuscript claim supported by these data is: independently analyzed rat bulk DRG studies show concordant enrichment of selected inflammatory and G2M-associated gene sets, with reduced oxidative-phosphorylation enrichment across oxaliplatin, NC and CCI contrasts. This is evidence of shared tissue-level transcriptional responses, not a cell-resolved mechanism.

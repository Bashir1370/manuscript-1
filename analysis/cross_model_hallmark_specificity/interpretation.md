# Interpretation of three-model Hallmark comparison

## Main finding

This analysis compares Hallmark pathway enrichment across three neuropathy models without merging transcriptomic matrices.

The comparison is performed at pathway level using independent GSEA outputs.

## Biological interpretation framework

### Shared neuropathy programs

Pathways enriched across OIPN, NC, and CCI represent conserved transcriptional responses associated with neuropathic injury.

These programs may reflect common responses of DRG tissue to different types of injury.

### OIPN-enriched programs

Pathways detected preferentially in OIPN should be interpreted as OIPN-associated transcriptional programs.

They may represent biological features related to chemotherapy-induced neuropathy, but pathway-level specificity does not establish causality.

### Physical injury-associated programs

Pathways enriched in NC and CCI but not OIPN may represent responses more closely related to mechanical nerve damage.

Examples may include immune and inflammatory programs associated with physical nerve injury.

## Limitations

This analysis compares independent datasets.

Therefore:

- raw expression matrices were not directly integrated.
- pathway specificity reflects enrichment differences between models.
- experimental design differences between studies should be considered during interpretation.

## Role in manuscript

This analysis provides a framework to distinguish conserved neuropathy biology from model-associated pathway programs.

# Reviewer feedback and response map

Source: reviewer comments supplied by the author in the conversation on 2026-09-28. This file is a structured summary, not a verbatim transcript.

## Reviewer 1: recommendation to reject

The central neuronal mechanism is unsupported by bulk DRG RNA-seq and bulk DRG qPCR. Dividing satellite glia, Schwann cells, and infiltrating immune cells provide an alternative explanation for mitotic and inflammatory transcripts. There is no cell-type localization, protein/activity evidence, or causal intervention. Intersecting oxaliplatin and nerve-compression datasets identifies a shared injury response, rather than oxaliplatin-specific biology.

## Reviewer 2: major concerns

1. **Shared versus OIPN-specific:** Common genes may indicate a general neuropathic-injury response. OIPN-only findings should not be labeled false positives; shared genes should not be labeled OIPN-specific drivers.
2. **Cellular localization:** Bulk DRG does not show whether Cdk1, Cdc20, and Cdkn1a are expressed in sensory neurons.
3. **Cell-cycle re-entry:** Increased mRNA is not functional evidence of cell-cycle activation or re-entry.
4. **Senescence:** Cdkn1a and pathway enrichment alone do not establish senescence.
5. **Causality:** Association of transcriptional and inflammatory changes with pain is not proof that these genes cause pain or are validated therapeutic targets.
6. **Temporal relationship:** Limited molecular time points do not establish the proposed sequence of cell-cycle change, arrest, senescence-like state, and inflammation.
7. **Alternative interpretation:** Gene expression may be secondary to injury; inflammation may be independent of cell-cycle-related expression.

## Response strategy without new mechanistic experiments

- Reframe the primary question as characterization of shared and differential **bulk DRG transcriptional responses**.
- Remove neuronal localization, cell-cycle activation, senescence, causal pain mechanisms, and therapeutic-target claims from the title, abstract, main conclusions, and graphical model.
- Test direction and effect size in each contrast, not only thresholded DEG overlap.
- Examine whether non-neuronal composition/state signatures offer an alternative interpretation; describe these analyses as exploratory and non-localizing.
- Explicitly acknowledge the single molecular time point and inability to infer causal order.
- Retain the independent rat behavioral and qPCR results as validation of phenotype and bulk mRNA expression only.
